Who We Are

Our Foundation

We are the former and current football players, coaches, administrators, and supporters of the Union of Liberian Footballers, protecting and promoting our shared interests. We work to enhance the welfare, dignity, and professional growth of Liberian footballers, foster unity, and uphold the enduring spirit of football — providing social, medical, and financial support to members while advancing integrity, good governance, and accountability across the sport.

What We Do

Aims & Objectives

a.

Protect and promote the rights, welfare, and professional interests of present and former Liberian footballers.

b.

Support grassroots football, and social, medical, education, pension, and emergency assistance programmes for members.

c.

Represent members with the Liberia Football Association, government bodies, and international football organisations.

d.

Facilitate training, career transition, networking, and community engagement for members.

e.

Heighten integrity, transparency, and good governance in all of the Union's affairs.

Governance

The Governing Structure

01

General Assembly of Members

The highest decision-making body of the Union, meeting every two years to elect national officers and approve programmes, reports, and the annual budget.

02

Elected Advisory Board

Provides oversight of the Executive Management Team and mobilises resources for the Union's programmes and projects. Nine elected members.

03

Executive Management Team

The principal administrative body, headed by the President, managing the day-to-day affairs of the Union.

04

Standing Committees

The strategic and operational arm of the Union, covering player welfare, finance, membership, elections, ethics, and records.

05

Local Chapters

Members across Liberia's districts, counties, and regions — supporting grassroots outreach and local events.
